# Launch the AI Native Factory: the model

The keynote's model holds here (`../augmented-pdlc/docs/model.md`: the wheel, the Nexus, the layers, the roles, the vocabulary and its banned variants, the sources). This deck retells it as a programme; what it adds is below. The lists are in `src/lists.js`, the stops as built in `docs/stop-map.md`.

## What the deck adds

- **The programme as a rocket.** Its three phases are the rocket's stages (step 2), the target a moon (steps 3, 21). The rocket wraps the engine: opened, it shows the engine inside (steps 5, 8).
- **Four pillars**, one section each: PDLC, Platform, Measure, People (step 5, the keynote's own words), plus the programme itself and the next steps.
- **The sets it visits** (the keynote's, `../augmented-pdlc/src/engine/sets.js`): the rocket on its pad, the cockpit for Measure (step 7: DORA's Stability and Throughput, the keynote's Quality, Transformation and Value), the contract dial (step 12), the classroom and mission control for training and engagement (steps 16 to 19).
- **The contract dial's zones**: the four delegation levels over a task's score, 0–100 (`zones`): Human, AI-assisted, AI-executed (drawn filled), AI-autonomous (hatched once a limit keeps the needle out of it). Step 12 reads a task's score, a blocking rule and the foundations' ceiling; 12.6 is the next level, human on the loop.
- **Seven AI capabilities** from DORA 2025 (the `concerns` list, step 17): a clear AI stance, healthy data, data AI can reach, strong version control, small batches, the user at the centre, a quality platform.
- **Flight Deck** (step 19): a fictional programme site drawn in the deck's own look (`assets/flight-deck/`, its README). Never a client's real tool.

## Sources

Faros AI 2026, DORA 2025, McKinsey 2025, each named in the HUD's source line. Durations are Zenika estimates, labelled as such; the measure families are DORA's two (Stability, Throughput) and the keynote's Quality, Transformation and Value; example scores are labelled illustrative.
