## Act: The wheel

**s03: A Six-Phase Product Delivery Life Cycle**
*Shot: top-right; parts: wheel; names: phases.*
Five phases form the loop: Discover, Design, Develop, Deploy, Deepen. Drop, the sixth, hangs outside it and retires what no longer earns its place.

**s04: One Idea's Journey in Twelve Shapes**
*Shot: top-right; parts: wheel; names: phases,shapes.*
It starts as an idea. Design shapes it into a spec. Develop turns the spec into code, builds it and reviews it. Deploy ships it and keeps it running. Deepen observes it in use and analyses the results.

**s05: A Gate on Every Passage**
*Shot: top-right; parts: wheel; in focus: gates,idea; names: gate-names,gate-notes.*
Between two phases, the idea stops at a gate. Machines check first: tests, scans, schemas, the criteria the phase promised. Then a named person decides, and the platform records the decision. · answers: govern,secure

**s07: Org, BU, Team: Nested**
*Shot: telescope-left; parts: wheel,nexus; in focus: nexus; names: levels.*
The Nexus follows the organisation's shape. The organisation sets what everyone inherits: security, conventions, shared tools. A business unit adds what its teams share, and a team adds its own domain. · answers: govern

**s08: Intent Belongs to the Team**
*Shot: nexus-top; parts: wheel,nexus; in focus: register:intent; names: register:intent.*
Three registers come down from above. Intent does not: it exists only at team level, because what to build is always a team's call. · answers: feed
