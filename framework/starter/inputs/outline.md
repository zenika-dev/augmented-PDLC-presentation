# The Starter Machine: a deck on the framework in three steps

> After 3 minutes, a deck author can say what a stop, a beat and a set are.

Audience: deck authors. Length: 3 minutes. Languages: en.
Sources: DORA 2025.
Questions:
- what: What it is | What is the machine made of?
- test: How it is tested | Where does each part get tested?

## Act 1: The machine

**s01: One Machine, Two Parts**
*The whole machine from above: a base plate, a core standing in it. Names on both parts.*
A base plate holds a core. Every step of a deck is a camera shot on one scene like this one, plus the state of its parts. · answers: what

**s02: The Core Lifts Out**
*Closer on the core as it rises out of the plate.*
A stop sets the scene's state: here the core rises out of the plate.
*Beat 2: the core turns to the energy colour.* · answers: what

## Act 2: The bench

**s03: A Set of Its Own**
*Cut to the test bench, a place of its own beside the machine.*
A set is a place of its own. Moving to it is a cut, unless a portal or a turntable joins the two places. · answers: test
